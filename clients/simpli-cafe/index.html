What we do

Three things, done properly

A small menu is a promise: everything on it gets the attention it deserves.

Coffee worth the walk

A single-origin espresso roasted for us in Athens, ground to order. Freddo, filter, flat white or a proper Greek coffee in the briki — however you take it.

Baked the same morning

Croissants, koulouri, tiropita and a tray of whatever the kitchen felt like at 6am. When it's gone, it's gone — which is rather the point.

A stool in the sun

Tables out on the pavement under the striped awning, wifi that works, and nobody hurrying you along. Stay for one, stay for the afternoon.

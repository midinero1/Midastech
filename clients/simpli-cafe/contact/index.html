Write to us

Anything else on your mind?

Large orders for the office, a birthday cake, a lost umbrella, or a note about something we could do better — it all comes to the same inbox, and we read it.

For catering and group bookings, tell us the date, how many of you there are and roughly what you'd like. We'll come back within a day.

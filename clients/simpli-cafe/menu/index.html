Milk, sugar, everything else

Oat, almond and lactose-free milk swap in for €0.40. An extra shot is €0.50. Decaf costs the same as the real thing, and takeaway cups are compostable — bring your own and take €0.20 off.

Allergies

VE marks what's vegan. Everything is made in a small kitchen that handles gluten, nuts, dairy and sesame, so tell whoever is on the counter and we'll talk you through the safest thing on the board.

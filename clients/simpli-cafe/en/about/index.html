About us

A small place, run by the people behind the counter

No head office, no franchise manual. Just a room on a corner, a machine we know inside out, and the same faces most mornings.

How it started

We took the shop over when it was still a shuttered kiosk, kept the yellow shutter because we liked it, and opened with an espresso machine, a fridge and eight stools. That was the whole plan.

The name came before anything else. We wanted a place that did a few things properly rather than everything adequately — good coffee, something fresh to go with it, and somewhere to sit for ten minutes before the day starts. Simple, but not careless. There's a difference, and you can taste it.

Years later the menu has grown by about six lines. The stools are the same ones.

What we care about

Three rules we haven't broken yet

Coffee we'd drink ourselves

One espresso blend, roasted for us in Attica and delivered every week. We dial it in each morning and taste it again after lunch.

Made here, not shipped in

The pies, the cakes and the sandwich fillings are made in our kitchen. Bread comes from a bakery four streets away.

Faces, not orders

We learn how you take it. If you're a regular you'll get a cup started before you've reached the counter.

The sign

One board, one afternoon

The sign over the pavement was cut from a single length of pine and left with its live edge on, so no two sides match. The letters were routed by hand.

There's a knot in the wood that landed exactly where the dot over the last i should go. Nobody planned it. We decided that was the logo and never drew another one — you'll find that same brown dot in every corner of this site.

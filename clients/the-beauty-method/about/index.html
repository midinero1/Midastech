About

A small treatment room, a short menu and an unhurried diary — built for people who want beauty work done properly rather than quickly.

Our story

Built around the appointment, not the schedule.

The Beauty Method started from a familiar frustration: appointments that run to the clock rather than to the face in front of them. Brows shaped to whatever was in fashion. Wax applied at whatever temperature the pot happened to be. A result that looked fine leaving the salon and wrong by the evening.

So the studio was set up the other way round. One guest at a time. A menu kept short enough to do every treatment on it genuinely well. Timings generous enough to include the consultation and the aftercare rather than treating them as extras.

The result is a room that feels more like a private appointment than a salon floor — quiet, warm, and entirely about the person in the chair.

What to expect

What the studio is like

No music you have to talk over, no queue behind you, and no treatment sold to you from the chair.

One guest at a time

The diary is deliberately small. Your appointment is not overlapped with anyone else's, which is why it never feels hurried at the end.

Hygiene without exception

Fresh tools and fresh wax for every guest, surfaces reset between appointments, and patch tests taken seriously rather than waived to save time.

Enhancement, not alteration

The brief is always the same: you, on a good day. If a treatment would not suit your features, we will say so and suggest what would.

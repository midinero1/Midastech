Before you come in

  • Tinting needs a patch test at least 24–48 hours before your first appointment, so book that in when you book the treatment.
  • For waxing, hair wants to be about a quarter of an inch — roughly two to three weeks of growth — and skin is happiest unexfoliated on the day.
  • Arrive with nails free of polish where you can. If not, removal is part of the appointment and simply uses a little of the time.
  • Every treatment ends with aftercare notes for the 48 hours that follow, and a sensible date to come back.

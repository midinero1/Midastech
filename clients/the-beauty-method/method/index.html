How We Work

Our method

The name is not decoration. There is an actual method — four steps that every appointment runs through, in the same order, whether it is a ten-minute brow tint or a full leg wax.

Why a method

Consistency is the luxury.

Anyone can have a good appointment once. What is hard is having the same one every time — the same shape, the same comfort, the same finish, regardless of the day of the week or how full the diary is.

So the studio works to a fixed sequence. It sets how long each treatment is booked for, what happens before the first strip of wax or stroke of tint, and what you leave knowing. Nothing about it is rushed, because the timings are built around the method rather than the other way round.

Enhancing your natural beauty

The Four Steps

Consult, prepare, refine, aftercare

01

Consult

Before anything is touched we talk about what you want and what your skin will tolerate — how much brow you want to keep, how dark a tint should read, whether anything has reacted before, what you have coming up that week.

It takes a few minutes and it is the step that prevents almost every disappointing result. Brow shapes in particular are mapped to your own bone structure here, not to a trend.

02

Prepare

Skin is cleansed and prepped, tints are patch-tested in advance, and tools come out fresh for you — no double dipping, no shortcuts between guests.

Most of how comfortable a treatment feels is decided here, before it begins. Wax is brought to the right temperature for your skin rather than left sitting at one setting all day.

03

Refine

The treatment itself, worked in small sections and checked as it goes. Waxing is taken in short strips with the skin supported, so it lifts hair rather than pulling at you.

Shape, symmetry and colour are reviewed while there is still room to adjust — which is the whole point of checking early rather than at the end.

04

Aftercare

Skin is soothed and calmed before you sit up, and you leave with the two or three things that actually matter for the next 48 hours — what to keep off the area, what to put on it, what is normal and what is not.

We will also tell you honestly when to come back. Sometimes that is four weeks; sometimes it is longer than you expected.

What Guides It

Three things we hold to

Skin before speed

No treatment is worth an angry face for two days. If your skin says today is not the day, we adapt the plan or reschedule — and we will say so.

Your face, your shape

Brows are mapped to your features, tint is mixed towards your own colouring. The aim is that nobody can name what you had done, only that you look well.

Nothing you did not ask for

No upselling from the chair, no packages you did not want. You booked a treatment; you get that treatment, done properly.

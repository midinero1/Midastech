Welcome

A small studio, and an unhurried one.

The Beauty Method is a boutique treatment room built around one idea: beauty work should be precise, calm and entirely yours. One guest at a time, no rushing, no upselling — just careful hands and a finish that still looks like you.

Every appointment begins with a proper look at what you actually want, and ends with the aftercare that keeps it looking that way. That sequence is the method, and it is the same whether you are here for a brow tint or a full leg wax.

Our Method

Four steps, every single time

The method is not a treatment on the menu. It is the order things happen in, and it does not change because the room is busy.

01

Consult

We start with your skin, your colouring and what you want it to look like on the day after — not a form and a stopwatch.

02

Prepare

Skin cleansed, patch tests where they are due, fresh tools every time. Most of the comfort in a treatment is decided before it starts.

03

Refine

The treatment itself, worked in small sections and checked as we go, so the shape is corrected while it can still be corrected.

04

Aftercare

Skin soothed, and you leave knowing exactly what to do for the next 48 hours and when to come back.
